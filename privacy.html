Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

This policy covers f0.io and overtones.f0.io, run by f0 Inc. (“f0”, “we”). It explains what we collect when you visit these sites or sign up for early access, why, and what you can do about it. Questions or requests: contact@f0.io.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you sign up for early access on f0.io. We use it only to send you news about f0 and early access. You can unsubscribe from any email we send, or ask us to delete it.
  • Usage analytics: the pages you view, the links you click, the site that sent you, your browser, device and screen size, and an approximate location (country or city) worked out from your IP address. We use this to see what people read and to make the sites better.
  • Technical logs: our host keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and browser) to deliver the sites and protect them from abuse.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Cookies

Until you choose, analytics runs without cookies: nothing is stored on your device, and a return visit can't be linked to an earlier one. If you click Accept, our analytics tool stores an identifier in a cookie and in your browser's local storage, so we can tell returning visitors apart and follow a visit across our sites. If you click Decline, it stays cookieless. Either way, we keep your choice in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again.

Who processes it for us

  • PostHog (PostHog, Inc., US): product analytics.
  • Loops (Loops.so, Inc., US): the early access list and the emails we send.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., US): hosting and delivery of both sites.

These providers handle data on our behalf and only as we instruct. Some of it is processed in the United States; where the law requires it, transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Why we're allowed to

For visitors in the EU, UK and similar places: we rely on your consent for the early access emails and for analytics cookies, and on our legitimate interest in understanding and securing our sites for cookieless analytics and logs. You can withdraw consent at any time; that doesn't affect what happened before.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We keep analytics data only as long as it's useful for understanding how the sites are used, and then delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or get a copy of it. Email contact@f0.io and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
